# MonstersDNA mascot

The master PNG was created with the built-in image-generation tool. All public
marks, favicons, Apple icons, and the Google OAuth logo derive from this artwork.
Regenerate format/size derivatives with `node apps/web/scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs`.
The opaque cream background is intentional and works on light or dark browser tabs.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: MonstersDNA game favicon, app icon and Google sign-in brand mark. Generate a single original polished mascot icon: a friendly moss-green monster head, with exactly three large warm-cream eyes arranged one above two, dark forest-green pupils, two short rounded amber-orange horns and a small happy mouth. Front-facing compact rounded silhouette, charming and curious, family-friendly. Simplified crisp flat illustration with only very subtle shading, bold shapes and strong contrast that remain readable at 32 pixels. Palette matches a lush island game: moss green, deep forest green, cream and warm orange. Center the head, including horns, within the central 80% of a square canvas. Genuinely transparent background, no tile, no surrounding scene, no letters, no text, no watermark, no mockups, no thin details or elaborate textures. Deliver one icon, not a grid.

## Final refinement prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the provided MonstersDNA mascot icon. Keep the exact same friendly three-eyed green monster, orange horns, face proportions, crisp illustration, composition and colors. Change ONLY the background: remove the entire fake checkerboard pattern and replace it with a perfectly uniform solid warm cream background, hex #FFF9E8, covering the complete square right up to every edge. Opaque background intentionally. Absolutely no checkerboard, no grid, no texture, no shadow behind the head, no text, no border or tile outline. One centered square favicon/app logo.
